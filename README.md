## Hi there 👋

I'm Alex. For most of my life, I've worked at the intersection of technology and storytelling.

I started in PR and marketing, working with global brands and startups across APAC. I learned quickly that great stories don't win because they're clever. They win because they land. Timing, incentives, and taste matter more than buzzwords.

Eventually, I got tired of watching great products struggle to get seen. So I started building tools instead of decks. Writing assistants, media databases and launch platforms designed to help builders earn attention without begging for it.

**Now, I build in public, [share numbers](https://alexmacgregor.com/mrr), and show the process. Not for optics, but for accountability. I often write about it on my [newsletter](https://newsletter.alexmacgregor.com/), [X](https://x.com/alexmacgregor__) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexmacgregor2/).**

Thanks for reading this. You can always contact me directly if you have any feedback at [alex@alexmacgregor.com](mailto:alex@alexmacgregor.com). I look forward to hearing from you.
